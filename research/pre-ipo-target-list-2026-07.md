# Targeted Pre-IPO Application List for a May 2026 CWRU CS/Data Science Grad

## TL;DR
- **The single best-fit tier is AI-native fintech-infrastructure and healthtech companies that run *explicit* university/new-grad software engineering programs and closed fresh rounds in 2025–2026 — led by Ramp, Watershed, Notion, Abridge, Mercury, OpenEvidence, and Hippocratic AI — where a candidate with three Amazon SDE internships plus real founder/CTO experience is an above-average applicant because these firms explicitly recruit "builders" and value shipping velocity over pedigree.**
- **Across 28 verified pre-IPO, venture-backed, non-defense companies, the biggest concentration of new-grad openings right now is in (1) AI-native fintech/spend management, (2) ambient-AI and clinical-decision healthtech, and (3) AI application infrastructure — the three funding waves driving 2025–2026 hiring; mission-driven options (healthtech, climate, edtech, workforce mobility) are plentiful and specifically flagged.**
- **Prioritize companies with a named "University Grad / New Grad" posting (Ramp, Notion, Databricks, Watershed, Hippocratic AI, Replit) first, then builder-culture growth-stage firms; treat Sword Health, Cohere Health, and Cityblock as lower-probability given senior-only or geographically constrained engineering hiring.**

## Selection Methodology
I screened for six weighted criteria: (1) a funding round within roughly the last 12–18 months (2025–2026), which signals runway and active hiring; (2) confirmed open entry-level/new-grad/junior roles in SWE, data, ML, or AI (or a realistic path for a strong new grad); (3) stack match to the candidate's full-stack + data + ML/AI skill set; (4) culture signals that reward a FAANG-intern + founder profile — "builder mindset," ownership, autonomy, fast shipping; (5) US-based or remote-US; and (6) a mild preference flag for mission/social-impact work, since the candidate values wellbeing-improving work and explicitly avoids defense/military. Defense, weapons, and military-application companies were excluded entirely. Every company below is verified as still private/pre-IPO with a recent, sourced funding round.

**Why this candidate is competitive broadly:** Three Amazon SDE internships put the candidate above the median new-grad applicant on raw engineering signal; the Forward Automations CTO role and Deepr (a16z portfolio) consulting demonstrate exactly the "builder/founder" quality that firms like Ramp, Mercury, Vercel, and Replit explicitly recruit for; and the data-science/AI/stats breadth opens ML/data roles at healthtech and AI-infra companies that many pure-CS grads can't target. The Cleveland Clinic MVP and ~400% productivity-gain track record are concrete, quantified impact stories that map directly onto healthtech (Abridge, Ambience, OpenEvidence) and enterprise-productivity (Notion, Glean, Ramp) narratives.

## Patterns Worth Noting
- **Three funding waves are driving new-grad hiring right now:** (1) **AI-native fintech / spend management** (Ramp, Mercury) — repeated mega-rounds at rising valuations and named university-grad programs; (2) **ambient-AI + clinical-decision healthtech** (Abridge, Ambience, OpenEvidence, Hippocratic AI) — driven by AI's dominance of digital-health funding: per Rock Health's "H1 2025 market overview," "For the first time, AI-enabled startups captured a majority (62%) of digital health venture funding in H1 2025" ($3.95B); and (3) **AI application infrastructure** (Sierra, Glean, Vercel, Databricks, Decagon, Graphite).
- **Where the *named* new-grad programs are:** Ramp, Notion, Databricks, Watershed, Hippocratic AI, and Replit have explicit "University Grad / New Grad" 2026 postings — the highest-probability applications. Innovaccer (INfusion) is the structured early-career route in healthtech.
- **Mission-driven density is highest in healthtech, climate, and edtech:** Watershed, Base Power (climate); Abridge, OpenEvidence, Hippocratic AI, Spring Health, Ambience, Camber, Sprinter (healthtech); Handshake, Guild, MagicSchool (edtech). The candidate can satisfy both hireability and values without compromising.
- **Builder/founder ethos is a genuine differentiator:** Ramp ("slope over intercept"), Mercury (founder-built), Vercel/Replit (developer-first), and Base Power ("what you've built, not the logos") explicitly reward exactly this candidate's profile.

## Recommendations
1. **Week 1 — Apply to the "named new-grad program" set first (highest conversion):** Ramp (University Grad SWE Backend/Frontend), Notion (New Grad SWE + New Grad AI), Databricks (SWE New Grad 2026), Watershed (SWE new grad), Hippocratic AI (Software Engineer New Grad), Replit (SWE New Grad Summer 2026). These have structured pipelines that reward FAANG-intern signal and lower the risk of résumé black-holing.
2. **Week 1–2 — Layer in the builder-culture growth-stage firms** where founder experience is the differentiator: Mercury, Vercel, Glean, Sierra, Rippling. Lead every application with the Forward Automations CTO story and the quantified ~400% productivity gain.
3. **Week 2 — Mission-driven push:** Abridge, OpenEvidence, Ambience (lead with the Cleveland Clinic MVP — Ambience has a Cleveland Clinic contract, making this a standout hook), Spring Health (data-platform roles), plus Watershed/Base Power for climate and Handshake/Guild/MagicSchool for edtech.
4. **Week 2–3 — Early-stage, high-ownership Series B bets** for maximum autonomy: Graphite, Decagon, Camber, Sprinter. Smaller teams mean a strong new grad ships immediately, matching the candidate's autonomy preference.
5. **Deprioritize but monitor:** Sword Health (US roles senior + no visa sponsorship), Cohere Health (senior-only engineering), Cityblock (limited junior tech; target Data Analyst II). Revisit if they post junior/new-grad roles.
6. **Benchmarks that would change this plan:** If a Tier-1 firm freezes new-grad hiring (watch for removed "University Grad" postings) or announces layoffs, shift weight down-tier. If the candidate secures a referral at any Tier-2/3 company, promote it — referrals outweigh tier for a builder profile. If a target files for IPO (watch Databricks, Mercury), pre-IPO equity upside changes but hireability generally stays intact.

## Caveats
- **Valuations move fast and some are markdowns or secondary-implied, not primary:** Ramp's valuation rose four times in 2025; Cityblock's $5.7B is a stale 2021 figure with a likely markdown; Cohere Health's valuation is undisclosed (ignore aggregator "$5.5B" claims). Treat all valuations as point-in-time.
- **Job postings expire.** Every "new-grad" title cited should be re-verified live on the company careers page before applying; several were captured in early-to-mid 2026.
- **Remote expectations vary sharply:** Hippocratic AI (~5 days on-site Palo Alto) and Base Power (100% in-person Austin) are effectively non-remote; Watershed and Notion expect heavy in-office presence; Cohere Health and Cityblock are the most genuinely remote-US.
- **Brex was reported (in one secondary source) to have been acquired by Capital One in a ~$5.2B deal in 2026;** because that would change its pre-IPO/independent status, Brex is intentionally excluded from the ranked list pending confirmation. **Chime is excluded because it completed its IPO in 2025** (now publicly traded). Both illustrate the need to re-confirm status at application time.
- **This list optimizes for fit and hireability, not for the "hottest" or highest-paying startups.** A few extremely high-bar AI firms were included (Sierra, Glean) because the candidate's profile is competitive, but expect long, selective loops there.
- **Defense/military excluded by design.** Some otherwise-attractive companies (e.g., Anduril, Palantir) were deliberately screened out to honor the candidate's explicit constraint; even Base Power's grid work was checked to ensure it is civilian energy infrastructure, not defense.
